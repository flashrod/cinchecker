How to read one

Twenty-one characters, six parts

Reliance Industries Limited's CIN, used here as a real example. Read left to right.

L Listing
17110 Industry
MH State / ROC
1973 Year
PLC Type
019786 Registration

The two-letter code is a registrar jurisdiction, not always a state abbreviation. Pune companies carry PN; both PN and MH decode to Maharashtra.

LLPIN

Shorter, and quieter

An LLPIN is AAA-1234: three letters, a hyphen, four digits. It carries no state, year, or company type, so those columns stay blank.

Limits

2,000 lines per check

Longer lists are cut at 2,000, and the number left out is shown. Nothing is uploaded; the check runs in this tab.

Not a registry

Format is not existence

A well-formed number can still be fake or struck off. Confirm the company on MCA21 before relying on it.
